# About IRONCREED

IRONCREED is a currently classified recurring character of [Zhovten Games](https://github.com/Zhovten-Games) and the personified engineering process behind its IT practice.

We connect development, infrastructure, automation, security, research and technical publishing into systems that can be understood, verified and changed deliberately.

---

## Focus

IRONCREED is the applied IT and engineering practice of Zhovten Games.

We build and maintain websites, applications, infrastructure and technical workflows while treating each project as a system of data, dependencies, rules, states and verifiable transitions.

Our work combines implementation with research, documentation and verification rather than separating them into unrelated stages.

---

## Experience

Our current public record includes **200+ documented high-density assignments** across web development, infrastructure, publishing, automation, product systems and technical operations.

Among them:

* **3+ years of continuous infrastructure support for EMBO Studio**, including parallel maintenance of 20+ internal websites.
* Academic publishing and typesetting work that contributed to the open **ZG Journal Template** pipeline.
* Product and content-system work connected with **Shifton** and **Zipy**.
* Infrastructure for services handling **hundreds of thousands of visits per day**, including Cloudflare-level traffic and crawler controls documented in the [Crawler Surface Policy case](https://web.zhovten.games/en/programming/cloudflare-001-crawler-surface-policy).
* Engineering mentorship and leadership within an international startup environment.

## Services

### DevOps & Infrastructure

* Web and application development.
* Deployment and environment management.
* Automation, testing and observability.
* Cloudflare and infrastructure operations.
* Security and configuration control.
* Reproducible release processes.

DevOps is treated as a way of organising the entire engineering effort rather than as a separate deployment stage.

### Corporate Knowledge for LLMs

* Private corporate knowledge repositories.
* Structured company profiles.
* Entity and relationship modelling.
* Access and data-boundary design.
* Integration with a client-selected LLM.
* Chat, specialised interfaces and existing-system integrations.

Knowledge architecture is kept independent from a specific AI provider so that the underlying corporate knowledge base can survive changes in models and vendors.

### Machine-Readable Websites

* Structured indexes of site materials.
* Explicit entities and relationships.
* Stable routes and metadata.
* AI-agent and corpus access.
* Semantic layers for authors, concepts, services, products, sources and other entities.

The objective is to make a website understandable not only to people, but also to search, agent and LLM systems.

### WARDEN

WARDEN is an independent verification boundary maintained alongside a project.

It can verify:

* route availability;
* configuration integrity;
* data structure;
* security rules;
* build correctness;
* regressions;
* project-specific invariants.

Established checks can block a release when required properties are no longer preserved.

## Strengths

* Treating products as systems rather than collections of isolated tasks.
* Connecting development, infrastructure, security and verification into one process.
* Building reusable engineering rules instead of accumulating one-off decisions.
* Designing machine-readable knowledge and publication structures.
* Turning practical findings into durable documentation, research and subsequent engineering changes.

---

## Material Cycle

Practical work feeds a recurring publication and verification process:

**Idea or IT case → Research and record → IRONCREED adaptation → Verification and the next change**

For example, research into software architecture rules produced the **[Code Constitution](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21894242)**, received a durable Zenodo record, and was later adapted into [The Constitution That Runs](https://web.zhovten.games/en/series/the-constitution-that-runs) publication series.

The resulting findings continue to inform engineering rules and subsequent project work.

The full process is documented on the **[Material Cycle](https://web.zhovten.games/en/pages/material-cycle)** page.
